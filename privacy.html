Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

NostalgOS is a local handheld cartridge player for iPhone and Mac. We do not run an account, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or telemetry service. We do not receive your cartridge files, saves, screenshots, library details, or usage history. NostalgOS does not track you across apps or websites.

Files stored on your device

When you import a .gb, .gbc, or .gba file, NostalgOS copies it into the app’s local storage. Battery saves, save states, screenshots, custom names, favorites, display preferences, and play time are also stored locally. NostalgOS needs these files to run cartridges and keep your progress. They remain until you remove them in the app or delete the app and its local data. Your device’s backup settings may also include app data in a device backup. NostalgOS also keeps up to five earlier battery saves per game, a copy of any save it replaces when you import or restore one, and, for 30 days, a copy of any save that sync deletes. These recovery copies stay on the device.

Save sync through iCloud or a custom folder

With NostalgOS Pro, new installations default to automatic iCloud save sync when iCloud is available. NostalgOS uses its own iCloud container for battery saves, save states, slot screenshots, and library metadata such as game names, favorites, appearance choices, and play time. ROMs and app settings are not uploaded by save sync. Apple transfers and stores this data under its iCloud terms; NostalgOS does not operate a server or receive copies of your saves. The core player works locally without an Apple Account.

You can turn iCloud Sync off in Settings. Advanced settings also offer a custom folder, using access granted by the system file picker. Files are kept in a NostalgOS Sync subfolder. If that folder is managed by another provider, the provider may transfer and store the files under its own terms. Existing custom-folder users keep that destination until they choose iCloud.

Migration retains a local backup and snapshots of the previous destinations before merging saves. The original custom folder is not deleted or modified by migration. These recovery copies remain on the device. File modification times are used to reconcile changes.

Turning sync off stops future synchronization and keeps local progress. It does not delete cloud copies, custom-folder files, exported backups, or migration backups. Manage iCloud app data through your Apple Account's iCloud storage settings; manage custom-folder files through Files, Finder, or the provider's app. Uninstalling the app does not automatically remove cloud or exported copies.

Screenshots, backups, and sharing

Sharing a screenshot or exporting a .nostalgos backup happens only when you choose a destination through the system share sheet or file exporter. A Back Up Library export can include your cartridge files; Back Up Saves Only omits them. The receiving app, person, or storage provider controls what happens after you share or export. On iPhone, choosing to save an image to Photos uses the system’s add-only access; NostalgOS does not read your photo library. On Mac, the Take Screenshot command writes to ~/Pictures/NostalgOS.

Optional purchase

Apple processes NostalgOS Pro purchases through the App Store. NostalgOS checks the purchase entitlement on your device and remembers the last known unlock state for offline use. We do not receive your payment credentials. Apple’s handling of purchase and account information is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Your choices and deletion

You can use the core player without an account or Pro purchase. You can stop save sync in Settings, choose whether to export or share files, remove individual cartridges and saves through the app where supported, and delete the app and its local data through your device. Deleting the app does not remove exported backups, shared screenshots, device backups, or files in a folder you selected outside the app. Delete those at their destinations if you no longer want them stored there.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question at github.com/harryw1/nostalgos/issues. Issues are public and need a GitHub account; don’t include personal details, ROM files or backups in them. GitHub’s handling of your account and anything you post there is covered by GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
